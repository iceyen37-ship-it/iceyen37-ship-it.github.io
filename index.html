2026Web app · Seniors · Behavior design

Fu'an Temple Earth God Health Station

Role: Design and development, solo

Many seniors in our neighborhood already walk to the Earth God temple every morning. Instead of asking them to exercise, I built on that habit: a mobile web app where the temple's Earth God counts your steps on the way, offers 3 routes graded by difficulty, asks a 10-question health quiz in plain language, and answers health questions by voice.

  • Step counting with the phone's motion sensor
  • Quiz results sent to a Google Sheets backend
  • Large text and voice input for older users

2026Mobile app · Caregivers · Research

SwallowCare

Role: Research, design and development

Families caring for seniors with chewing and swallowing difficulties are told to follow IDDSI, an international food texture standard written for clinicians. I interviewed 20 family caregivers using a Triple Diamond service design process, then turned the standard into tools they can use at home: visual texture cards, cooking demos, family recipes saved by texture level, a daily care log and a profile card for each senior.

  • 20 caregiver interviews
  • First-author poster, Asia IDDSI Conference 2026
  • Runs in the browser and as an Android app

2026UX research · Healthcare · Prototype

Hospital registration redesign

Role: Team lead, research and prototype design

I led a team studying National Taiwan University Hospital's online registration system. I reached out to the hospital, ran our team meetings and designed the surveys; together we tested the system with 5 people, from a 58-year-old homemaker to healthcare professionals. The system assumed medical knowledge: patients had to know which department to pick, and symptom search used medical terms. I designed a prototype with one entry point and plain-language symptom search. We sent our report to the hospital, which forwarded it to the relevant departments for future planning.

  • 5 usability tests
  • Report forwarded inside the hospital
  • Independent research, not affiliated with the hospital

2025 – nowCommunity health · Service design · Illustration

Warm Voyagers: walking maps for my hometown

Role: Design lead in a 3-person team

With two physical therapists I grew up with, I turned a 2.8 km riverside trail in Keelung into a health walk for seniors. I designed the illustrated map, posters and brand, and revised the map through more than 20 versions until residents could read it easily. In 2026 we expanded to family caregivers, with a trail safety map that rates every entrance for wheelchair access.

  • National Gold Award, 1 of 3 teams
  • 40 people at 2 guided walks, 100% satisfied
  • Selected again for 2026
